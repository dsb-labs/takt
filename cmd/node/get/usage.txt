Show the node.

Reports what the machine is: its hostname, kernel, processor count, the
version of takt serving the request, and when the process started and the
host booted. Reports what it has: memory, load averages, and the free space
under the data directory and the volumes directory.

The capacity figures are the host's. Memory in use and the load averages
cover everything on the box, not only takt's workloads, which report their
own usage on "takt workload get".
