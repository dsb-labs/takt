Show what a score contains once rendered, applying nothing.

Reports the install name and release, the volumes, workloads and services
the score would apply, the variables it sets and the ones it requires, the
secrets it declares, and the principals its workloads assert through token
references. The last two are what to check before an apply: every declared
secret must be set, and the access policy must grant each principal what
its workload needs.

Rendering needs no server, so this works anywhere the score does.

  takt score show ./blog
  takt score show ./blog --values prod.yaml --as blog-prod
