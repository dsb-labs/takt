Create or update a service from a manifest file.

The stored selection becomes what the manifest says, however many times
it is applied. The workloads the target selects do not have to exist: a
service applied ahead of its workloads reports no backends until they
arrive.

Pass --if-match the tag "takt service get" reported for the service
being replaced, and the apply is refused when the service has moved on
since, rather than writing over whatever landed in between. Without it
an apply writes unconditionally, which is what creating one has to do:
there is no tag yet to name.
