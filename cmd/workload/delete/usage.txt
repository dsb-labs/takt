Delete a workload and stop its work.

Deletion is asynchronous: the workload is reported as terminating while its
instances are stopped, and disappears once nothing is left running for it.
Pass --wait to block until the teardown has finished.

A workload another one references is refused, and the error names the workloads
reading its address. Pass --force to delete it anyway. Those workloads are then
redeployed and report the reference they can no longer resolve.
